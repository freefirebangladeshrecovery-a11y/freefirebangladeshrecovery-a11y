## About

I'm **Ahameed**, a content creator from **Bangladesh** behind the channel
[**W4NTED 404**](https://www.youtube.com/@W4NTED404). I make action-packed gameplay,
walkthroughs, tips and tricks for the games I play, alongside real-life vlogs and
lifestyle content.

The channel runs on a simple principle: **action-packed gameplay, honest walkthroughs, and
relatable moments from daily life** — published consistently, one video at a time.
